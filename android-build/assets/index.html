🛡️ Sensitive Population Protocols
  • Asthma / Respiratory Patients: Keep rescue bronchodilator inhalers within arm's reach. Never skip maintenance corticosteroid inhalers.
  • Elderly & Heart Patients: Completely suspend morning outdoor walks. Inhalation of particulate matter causes systemic vasoconstriction.
  • Children & Infants: Higher respiratory rates per body mass mean greater relative alveolar burden; restrict outdoor play.
